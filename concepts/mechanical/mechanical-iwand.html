iWand · no-electronics concept

Mechanical iWand

A colour identifier for colour-blind users that needs no battery and no circuit. Your eye only has to judge when two halves look equally bright, which colour-blind people can do. Optics and a printed card do the rest.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Orange paper, start to finish

  1. Match all three fields (Fig. 2). Readings: R 0.05, G 0.55, B 1.35.
  2. Spread between brightest and dimmest is 1.30, far above 0.03. It has a hue.
  3. Brightest is R at 0.05, in the bright zone. Not Black, not Brown.
  4. Order is R>G>B, the 0–60° slice: Red, Orange or Yellow.
  5. G sits 0.50 below R. The card says that is about 27°, so the colour is Orange.
